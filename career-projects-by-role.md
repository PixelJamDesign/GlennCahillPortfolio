**Design System Engineer (Contract) · June 2025 – present**

1. **CleanSlate architecture** — foundation, core, primitives, tokens, variables, typography, styling, spacing, baseline and rhythm ✅
2. **Component library** — feature-rich and prop-rich, built on atomic design methodology, renamed internally to *ingredients and recipes* ✅
3. **Ongoing system work** — iconography and illustration from the brand teams, email templates, baseline strategies for key business stakeholders ✅
4. **React Native implementation** — all components coded to a single consistent codebase across iOS, Android and web ✅
5. **Rapid prototyping pipeline** — Code Connect and Claude AI against the live codebase, shortening time to market and to UX testing ✅
6. **Recipe library** *(planned)* — a pattern library of templates for building and prototyping at pace

**Team:** four designers. Glenn built the system; three senior designers contributed assets and sense-checked.

- **Communicating research to stakeholders** (Centrica) and **training internal teams** (CIBC) are the two entries showing work directed upward and outward rather than screen production.
- **A career pivot sits at July 2021** — permanent to contract, product design to design systems. BMI is where it starts.
